TOPCon vs Mono PERC Panels: Which Delivers Higher Yield in Indore Summer Heat?

Why cell temperature coefficients matter when Indore temperatures hit 44°C+. An engineering analysis comparing N-Type Tunnel Oxide Passivated Contact (TOPCon) with older P-Type Mono PERC technology.

The Physics: Solar Panels Hate Extreme Heat

All solar panels are rated at standard test conditions (STC) of 25°C cell temperature. However, on a typical May afternoon in Indore when ambient air is 43°C, the dark silicon cells baking under direct sunlight frequently exceed 65°C to 70°C. How much power you lose above 25°C is governed exclusively by the temperature coefficient of Pmax.

Technical Head-to-Head Comparison

The Real Thermal Math at 65°C Operating Cell Temp

When the cell temperature reaches 65°C (which is 40°C above standard 25°C):

  • Mono PERC Heat Loss: 40°C × 0.38% = 15.2% power loss. A 550W panel generates only 466 Watts.
  • N-Type TOPCon Heat Loss: 40°C × 0.30% = 12.0% power loss. A 550W panel maintains 484 Watts.
  • Net Generation Advantage: That is a direct 3.2% to 4.5% extra generation during peak midday hours in Indore from thermal coefficient alone!

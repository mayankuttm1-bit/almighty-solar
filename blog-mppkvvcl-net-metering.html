What is Net-Metering & Why Is It Critical?

Net-metering is the regulatory mechanism governed by the Madhya Pradesh Electricity Regulatory Commission (MPERC). It permits a solar energy generator to export surplus daytime power back into the MPPKVVCL distribution grid. A bi-directional smart meter registers electricity imported from the grid and solar power exported to the grid, billing you solely for the net difference.

MPPKVVCL Net-Metering Eligibility Criteria

Sanctioned Load Rule

In Madhya Pradesh, rooftop solar capacity is permitted up to 100% of the consumer's sanctioned load (connected load) as specified on your MPPKVVCL electricity bill. If you need a larger solar array, load extension can be processed online concurrently.

Transformer (DTR) Limit

Total cumulative solar capacity installed under any local Distribution Transformer (DTR) cannot exceed 80% of rated transformer capacity. Early application secures your transformer capacity allocation.

Anti-Islanding Safety

All inverters must be certified to IEC 62109 and IEC 61727 with automatic anti-islanding trip protection within 2.0 seconds to safeguard MPPKVVCL linesmen working during feeder outages.

Bi-Directional Meter Class

For single-phase LT connections up to 5 kW, 1-Phase Class 1.0 smart bi-directional meters apply. For 3-Phase loads (above 5 kW), Class 0.5S/1.0 3-Phase 4-Wire DLMS/COSEM meters are mandatory.

The 6 Steps to MPPKVVCL Net-Metering Commissioning

Step 1: Online Application Submission

Submission of application on the National Rooftop Solar Portal or MPPKVVCL portal with consumer IVRS number, proposed system size (kW), and single-line diagram (SLD).

Step 2: Technical Feasibility Approval (TFA)

The MPPKVVCL Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) and Junior Engineer (JE) inspect the feeder capacity and approve feasibility within 7 working days.

Step 3: Solar Plant Construction & Earthing Verification

Almighty Solar installs the array, inverters, ACDB/DCDB panels, and dual chemical earthing pits (array + inverter) maintaining earth resistance below 3 ohms.

Step 4: Meter Testing at Discom Laboratory

The approved bi-directional energy meter is submitted to the MPPKVVCL Meter Testing Division (Indore) for calibration and accuracy verification under Discom test norms.

Step 5: Joint Site Inspection & Physical Meter Installation

MPPKVVCL representatives and Almighty Solar engineers conduct physical synchronisation, calibrate anti-islanding trip relays, and seal the new bi-directional meter.

Step 6: Joint Commissioning Report (JCR) Issuance

The signed Joint Commissioning Report (JCR) is uploaded to the portal, initiating automatic monthly energy banking on your bill and releasing the Central DBT subsidy.

How Energy Banking Works on Your Bill

Under MPERC regulations:

  • Excess Daytime Export: When solar generates 25 units in daytime and you consume 10 units, 15 units are credited into your energy bank.
  • Nighttime Import: At night, when you consume 12 units from the grid, these are drawn from your solar bank. Your bill reflects zero net units!
  • Carry Forward: Surplus units at month-end carry forward to subsequent months until the annual settlement cycle in March.

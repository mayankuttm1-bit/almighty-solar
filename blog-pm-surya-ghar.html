5-Step Process to Claim Your Subsidy in Indore

1

National Portal Registration

Register on pmsuryaghar.gov.in by selecting Madhya Pradesh and Discom MPPKVVCL (West Discom Indore). Enter your 10-digit Consumer IVRS Number from your latest electricity bill.

2

Technical Feasibility Approval (TFA)

MPPKVVCL verifies your local distribution transformer (DTR) capacity. Almighty Solar Energy coordinates with the junior engineer (JE) to secure feasibility clearance in 3–5 working days.

3

Empaneled Installation Execution

Almighty Solar installs MNRE ALMM Listed N-Type TOPCon panels, BIS-certified grid-tied inverter, hot-dip galvanized mounting structures, chemical earthing, and lightning arresters.

4

Net-Meter Testing & Joint Commissioning Report (JCR)

A dual bi-directional smart meter is tested in the MPPKVVCL laboratory, installed at your premises, and an official Joint Commissioning Report is signed and uploaded to the portal.

5

Direct Subsidy Credit into Bank Account (DBT)

Submit your bank account details and cancelled cheque on the portal. The Central Government transfers ₹78,000 directly via PFMS DBT within 25–30 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the entire subsidy process take in Indore?

From the day of physical installation, MPPKVVCL net meter commissioning takes about 10–14 days. Once the JCR is uploaded, the subsidy of ₹78,000 reaches your bank account within 25–30 days.

Can I install solar on a rented house or shop?

The PM Surya Ghar subsidy is specifically intended for residential domestic consumers (tariff category LV-1). For commercial properties, 40% Accelerated Tax Depreciation applies instead under Income Tax Act Section 32.

Does Almighty Solar handle all paperwork with MPPKVVCL?

Yes, 100% turnkey. We prepare the drawings, submit the online application, coordinate the meter testing, and track your DBT payment until it is safely received in your bank.

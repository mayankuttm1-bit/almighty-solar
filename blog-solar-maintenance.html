The Soiling Loss Problem in Indore

In Central India, the dry period spans nearly 8 months (October through May). With extensive infrastructure activity across the Super Corridor, Bypass, and industrial zones like Sanwer Road, airborne particulate matter coats the solar glass with a stubborn cement-like film. Field data recorded by Almighty Solar indicates an unwashed system loses 1.5% to 2.2% generation every single week, adding up to an 18% to 22% monthly revenue deficit!

4 Golden Rules for Cleaning Solar Panels

1. Clean Early Morning or Late Evening

Never spray cold water onto hot glass at midday (when glass temperature exceeds 65°C). The resulting thermal shock can shatter tempered glass or cause micro-cracks across silicon cells. Clean before 8:00 AM or after 5:30 PM.

2. Use Soft Demineralized Water

Indore ground borewell water often has high Total Dissolved Solids (TDS > 500 ppm). Using hard water leaves chalky white calcium scale deposits that permanently block light. Use rainwater, RO water, or soft tap water.

3. Soft Microfiber & Zero Detergents

Never use abrasive nylon brushes, metallic scrapers, or harsh chemical detergents. These destroy the specialized anti-reflective coating (ARC) on solar glass. Use soft microfibers and specialized rubber squeegees.

4. Never Walk or Step on Modules

Stepping on modules causes invisible cell micro-fractures. Over time, localized hotspots develop at cracked boundaries, creating diode burnout and permanent array power drop.

The Pithampur & Sanwer Road Energy Dilemma

Industrial units in Pithampur SEZ (Sectors 1, 2, and 3) and Sanwer Road operate under high-tension (HT-1 or HT-2) tariffs. Factoring in contract demand penalties, reactive energy surcharges, time-of-day (TOD) peak tariffs, and electricity duty, the landed cost per unit consistently exceeds ₹8.50 to ₹9.40 per kWh.

For an auto-ancillary or plastics plant consuming 35,000 units monthly, energy accounts for up to 22% of total operating overheads. Turnkey rooftop solar EPC transforms this recurring OPEX into a tax-advantaged capital asset.

Engineering Standards for Factory Sheds

Non-Penetrating Klip-Lok Clamps

Factory sheds with standing seam or trapezoidal profile sheets are fitted with anodized aluminum non-penetrating clamps. Zero roof piercing guarantees 100% leak-proof integrity even in heavy monsoons.

Zero Export & DG Synchronization Relays

For plants running heavy diesel generators during feeder outages, our smart micro-controllers modulate inverter generation to maintain generator load above 35%, preventing reverse power engine trips.

Three-Phase Multi-MPPT Inverters with Surge Arresters

Sungrow and Solis string inverters with Class-II DC & AC surge protection devices ensure protection against industrial voltage spikes, harmonic distortion, and lightning strikes.
